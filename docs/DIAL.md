# Category dial launcher (experimental)

A disc centred on the clock pill that slides down from the top edge of the screen (360 ms, no overshoot; the prototype scales it out of the pill): categories on an inner ring, apps on an outer arc. It sits beside the classic dock launcher; the Launcher Style setting (`launcherStyle`: `classic` or `dial`) picks which one opens.

- **Open:** set Settings → Launcher → Launcher Style to Dial. Then left-click on the clock pill, the dock's launcher button, the launcher shortcut and `ipc call launcher toggle` open the dial, and right-click on the pill expands the island. With Classic they open the classic launcher and either click expands the pill. `ipc call dial toggle` opens the dial whatever the style, and `ipc call launcher windows` opens it on Windows. An alerting notification still opens on click.
- **Screen:** the dial opens on one screen only (`DialService.screenName`): the one whose pill or dock was clicked, else the active window's. A press outside the disc closes it.
- **Files:** `dial/dialLogic.js` (geometry, categories, lists; tested by `tests/tst_dialLogic.qml`), `dial/DialService.qml` (state), `dial/DialWindow.qml` (view, one per screen), `dial/DialMenu.qml` (tile menu).
- **Turn:** wheel inside 288 px of the pill centre turns the ring, outside it the arc. One wheel notch is one step and touchpad deltas add up (`wheelSteps`), so a fast scroll turns further. The turn takes 360 ms for one step and 40 ms more for each further step, up to 900 ms, so a long turn spins faster. Left/Right move along the arc, Home/End go to its ends and PageUp/PageDown four tiles along, Up/Down change category, Enter launches, Ctrl+P pins, the Menu key or Shift+F10 opens the tile's menu, Esc closes.
- **Grab:** press on the ring or the arc and drag round the pill to turn it by hand; the selection follows. Let go while moving and it spins on by a quarter second of its speed (`flickTarget`), then settles on a slot. A press that moves less than 8 px is a click. Only the rings can be grabbed, from 200 px of the centre out (the grab layer's `containmentMask`); inside that the search field and the pill take presses. The cursor is an open hand over the rings.
- **Spinner:** each ring's turn is a `Spinner` in `DialWindow.qml`, a position in steps that glides to the selection or follows the pointer. Tiles and pills take their place, opacity and upright angle from it, not from the selection.
- **Windows:** the second category lists every open window (`DockService.windowItems`, as the classic launcher's Windows tab). A window tile shows its title and app; choosing it switches to it.
- **Running apps:** a dot under the tile marks an app with an open window (`DialService.runningIds`, from the desktop entry the dock finds for each window). Choosing a running app switches to its window, the next one after the active window if it has several, instead of starting it again; New Window in its menu starts another. In search, running apps come first among the name matches.
- **Menu:** right-click a tile. Apps: Switch To and New Window (running) or Open, the app's own actions (`DockService.getActionsForApp`), Pin to Dial, Edit Application… and Quit (running). Windows: Switch To and Close Window. Files: Open and Open Containing Folder. Settings: Open. The entries come from `DialService.menuFor`, drawn by `dial/DialMenu.qml`; Up/Down and Enter work in it.
- **Pins:** `dial_pinned.json` (gitignored), a list of app ids. Separate from the dock's pins.
- **Recent and Frequent:** read from the dock launcher's `launcher_usage.json` (`DockService.usageStats`), 16 each. A dial launch is recorded there too.
- **Launch:** goes through `WindowService.launchEntry`. Terminal apps are wrapped in `konsole -e`.
- **Search:** typing in the field under the pill swaps the category ring for four scopes with counts (Everything, Apps, Settings, Files). Everything lists apps first, then settings and files (prefix matches first among those). The best hit sits in the bottom slot, the rest alternate right and left. Up/Down change scope, Esc clears the text and then closes. Ranking is name matches before generic-name ones, then running, pinned and most launched apps first, then name prefix before substring (`matchApps`).
  - Settings come from `SettingsPagesService` (two letters or more), the same pages as the dock launcher's column.
  - Files come from `FileSearchService` (Filehouse), three letters or more, after 150 ms. They search whether or not the dock launcher's File Search setting is on, and the Files scope is empty if Filehouse is missing. The files of the last search stay on the arc until new ones arrive, and a file is only opened when its results belong to the text typed.
  - Only apps can be pinned.
- **Style:** category pills are bent along the ring, drawn as a `Shape`, with the label set one letter at a time. They are spaced by width, 10 px apart (`ringAngles`), not at the prototype's fixed 22°, so long labels do not crowd; widths are measured in bold so the spacing holds when the selection moves. Tiles and pills lighten under the pointer. The scrollbar thumb follows the arc as it turns. A thin border circle separates the ring from the arc, under the scrollbar. The disc is frosted glass with window blur when the dock style is glass and blur is available, otherwise solid. Tiles are only created within six steps of the bottom slot.
- **Status icons (test):** with Settings → Launcher → Status Icons in Dial (`dialStatusIcons`) on and the Dial style, the status icons sit under the search field instead of the top-right corner: system icons, then a thin line and the optional ones (Hue, Night Light…). They are the same buttons as the corner's (`island/StatusIconSet.qml`, hosted by the `statusIcons` column in `DialWindow.qml`). The corner shows the icons only while one of its own menus is open, such as the notification list from the notification bubble.
- **Pages:** an icon listed in `PAGE_OF_ICON` (`dialLogic.js`) opens a page of the dial instead of a menu: the ring, the arc and the middle change over, with the tiles and pills spinning in (`DialService.page`). Esc, the pill or the icon again go back to the launcher. On a page typing does nothing; the arrows, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown and Enter work as on the launcher. Arc tiles of kind `action` show an icon in a disc of their `tint`; a `confirm` action turns red with "Click again" on the first click and runs on a second within 3 s (`armedAction`), and a `toggle` action flips a setting and leaves the dial open.
  - **Power** (battery and profile icons): the power profiles on the ring (one "Power" pill without power-profiles-daemon), the battery level as a gauge on the border circle (green charging, orange at 20% or less, red at 10% or less) and the level and status in the middle. The arc holds 60 Hz on battery (toggle), Settings, Lock (the starting slot), Sleep, Restart and Shut Down (both confirm).
  - **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi Off and Wi-Fi On on the ring, the connected network's signal on the gauge, its name and status in the middle. The arc holds Settings, Rescan, then the networks as `NetworkService` lists them, starting on the connected one. Choosing a network connects to it with `nmcli` and the dial stays open; there is no password entry, so only saved and open networks connect.
  - A page's middle and gauge come from `pageView` in `DialWindow.qml`, its ring and arc from `ringLabels`, `ringIndex`, `setRing` and `items` in `DialService`, and its tiles' behaviour from `runAction` and `actionOn`.
  - Other icons still open their old menu as a card over the disc below the pill (`statusMenu`). A press outside the card, Esc, the grabber or the icon again closes it but not the dial; while it is open the rings do not turn. The gear opens the settings at the status icons.
- **Scale:** every length goes through `Theme.px` and every font through `Theme.fontPx`, so the dial follows `uiScale` live. Checked by hand from 0.80 to 1.20.
- **Not built yet:** reduced-motion behaviour, which the shell does not have either.
