# Category dial launcher (experimental)

A disc centred on the clock pill that slides down from the top edge of the screen (360 ms, no overshoot; the prototype scales it out of the pill): categories on an inner ring, apps on an outer arc. It sits beside the classic dock launcher; the Launcher Style setting (`launcherStyle`: `classic` or `dial`) picks which one opens.

- **Open:** set Settings → Launcher → Launcher Style to Dial. Then left-click on the clock pill, the launcher shortcut and `ipc call launcher toggle` open the dial, and right-click on the pill expands the island. The dock hides its launcher button and the divider after it (`showLauncher` in `dock/DockBar.qml`). With Classic they open the classic launcher and either click expands the pill. `ipc call dial toggle` opens the dial whatever the style, and `ipc call launcher windows` opens it on Windows. An alerting notification still opens on click.
- **Screen:** the dial opens on one screen only (`DialService.screenName`): the one whose pill or dock was clicked, else the active window's. A press outside the disc closes it.
- **Files:** `dial/dialLogic.js` (geometry, categories, lists and the first status pages; tested by `tests/tst_dialLogic.qml`), `dial/dialPages.js` (the later status pages; tested by `tests/tst_dialPages.qml`), `dial/DialService.qml` (state), `dial/DialWindow.qml` (view, one per screen), `dial/DialMenu.qml` (tile menu).
- **Turn:** wheel inside 288 px of the pill centre turns the ring, outside it the arc. One wheel notch is one step and touchpad deltas add up (`wheelSteps`), so a fast scroll turns further. The turn takes 360 ms for one step and 40 ms more for each further step, up to 900 ms, so a long turn spins faster. Left/Right move along the arc, Home/End go to its ends and PageUp/PageDown four tiles along, Up/Down change category, Enter launches, Ctrl+P pins, the Menu key or Shift+F10 opens the tile's menu, Esc closes.
- **Grab:** press on the ring or the arc and drag round the pill to turn it by hand; the selection follows. Let go while moving and it spins on by a quarter second of its speed (`flickTarget`), then settles on a slot. A press that moves less than 8 px is a click. Only the rings can be grabbed, from 200 px of the centre out (the grab layer's `containmentMask`); inside that the search field and the pill take presses. The cursor is an open hand over the rings.
- **Spinner:** each ring's turn is a `Spinner` in `DialWindow.qml`, a position in steps that glides to the selection or follows the pointer. Tiles and pills take their place, opacity and upright angle from it, not from the selection.
- **Windows:** the second category lists every open window (`DockService.windowItems`, as the classic launcher's Windows tab). A window tile shows its title and app; choosing it switches to it.
- **Running apps:** a dot under the tile marks an app with an open window (`DialService.runningIds`, from the desktop entry the dock finds for each window). Choosing a running app switches to its window, the next one after the active window if it has several, instead of starting it again; New Window in its menu starts another. In search, running apps come first among the name matches.
- **Menu:** right-click a tile. Apps: Switch To and New Window (running) or Open, the app's own actions (`DockService.getActionsForApp`), Pin to Dial, Edit Application… and Quit (running). Windows: Switch To and Close Window. Files: Open and Open Containing Folder. Settings: Open. The entries come from `DialService.menuFor`, drawn by `dial/DialMenu.qml`; Up/Down and Enter work in it.
- **Pins:** `dial_pinned.json` (gitignored), a list of app ids. Separate from the dock's pins.
- **Recent and Frequent:** read from the dock launcher's `launcher_usage.json` (`DockService.usageStats`), 16 each. A dial launch is recorded there too.
- **Launch:** goes through `WindowService.launchEntry`. Terminal apps are wrapped in `konsole -e`.
- **Search:** typing in the field under the pill swaps the category ring for four scopes with counts (Everything, Apps, Settings, Files). Everything lists apps first, then settings and files (prefix matches first among those). The best hit sits in the bottom slot, the rest alternate right and left. Up/Down change scope, Esc clears the text and then closes. Ranking is name matches before generic-name ones, then running, pinned and most launched apps first, then name prefix before substring (`matchApps`).
  - Settings come from `SettingsPagesService` (two letters or more), the same pages as the dock launcher's column.
  - Files come from `FileSearchService` (Filehouse), three letters or more, after 150 ms. They search whether or not the dock launcher's File Search setting is on, and the Files scope is empty if Filehouse is missing. The files of the last search stay on the arc until new ones arrive, and a file is only opened when its results belong to the text typed.
  - Only apps can be pinned.
- **Style:** category pills are bent along the ring, drawn as a `Shape`, with the label set one letter at a time. They are spaced by width, 10 px apart (`ringAngles`), not at the prototype's fixed 22°, so long labels do not crowd; widths are measured in bold so the spacing holds when the selection moves. Tiles and pills lighten under the pointer. The scrollbar thumb follows the arc as it turns. A thin border circle separates the ring from the arc, under the scrollbar. The disc is frosted glass with window blur when the dock style is glass and blur is available, otherwise solid. Tiles are only created within six steps of the bottom slot.
- **Status icons (test):** with Settings → Launcher → Status Icons in Dial (`dialStatusIcons`) on and the Dial style, the status icons sit under the search field instead of the top-right corner: system icons, then a thin line and the optional ones (Hue, Night Light…). They are the same buttons as the corner's (`island/StatusIconSet.qml`, hosted by the `statusIcons` column in `DialWindow.qml`). The corner shows the icons only while one of its own menus is open, such as the notification list from the notification bubble.
- **Pages:** an icon listed in `PAGE_OF_ICON` (`dialLogic.js`) opens a page of the dial instead of a menu: the ring, the arc and the middle change over, with the tiles and pills spinning in (`DialService.page`). Esc, the pill or the icon again go back to the launcher. On a page typing does nothing; the arrows, Home/End, PageUp/PageDown and Enter work as on the launcher. Arc tiles of kind `action` show an icon in a disc of their `tint`; a `confirm` action turns red with "Click again" on the first click and runs on a second within 3 s (`armedAction`), and a `toggle` action flips a setting and leaves the dial open.
  - **Power** (battery and profile icons): the power profiles on the ring (one "Power" pill without power-profiles-daemon), the battery level as a gauge on the border circle (green charging, orange at 20% or less, red at 10% or less) and the level and status in the middle. The arc holds 60 Hz on battery (toggle), Settings, Lock (the starting slot), Sleep, Restart and Shut Down (both confirm).
  - **Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi Off and Wi-Fi On on the ring, the connected network's signal on the gauge, its name and status in the middle. The arc holds Settings, Rescan, then the networks as `NetworkService` lists them, starting on the connected one. Choosing a network connects to it with `nmcli` and the dial stays open. A secured network that is not saved (`NetworkService.savedSsids`, Wi-Fi connections named after their SSID) asks for its password first: the middle shows "Join <name>" and a password field, Enter connects and Esc cancels. The password goes to `nmcli --ask` on stdin, not on its command line. A failed connect shows "Could not connect to <name>" until the next one.
  - **Bluetooth:** Bluetooth Off and Bluetooth On on the ring, the battery of the first connected device that reports one on the gauge, the connected device's name and status in the middle. The arc holds Settings, then the paired devices (connected first, starting on the first connected one). Choosing a device connects or disconnects it and the dial stays open. Pairing new devices is left to the Settings tile.
  - **Hardware:** CPU, Memory and Temperature on the ring pick the reading in the middle and on the gauge (orange at 75%, red at 90%; for temperature above 60 and 75 °C, with the gauge full at 100 °C), with the other two on the line below. The arc holds System Monitor (dimmed without `plasma-systemmonitor`), then the eight busiest programs, by memory under Memory and by CPU otherwise, each under its app's name and icon when a desktop entry matches. A program's processes count as one (`scripts/top_processes.py`, sampled every 2 s only while the page is open; its core is tested by `tests/test_top_processes_core.py`). Choosing a program opens its menu: End (SIGTERM to every process of that name, not offered for the desktop's own, `PROTECTED_PROGRAMS`) and Open System Monitor.
  - **Microphone:** Muted and Live on the ring, the input level on the gauge (red when live, grey when muted) and in the middle, with the input's name below. The gauge is a slider here: press or drag on it (16 px either side, `SLIDER_BAND`) to set the level, or wheel over it for 5% steps; raising it unmutes. A knob marks the level. The arc holds Settings (`kcm_pulseaudio`), then the inputs, starting on the one in use; choosing one makes it the default input. Right-click on the icon still mutes.
  - **Night Light:** Night Light Off and On on the ring, the night temperature in the middle and on the gauge, which is a slider (1000 to 6500 K in steps of 100; one wheel step is 100 K). The screen shows the temperature as the slider moves and it is saved half a second after the slider stops, because saving reloads KWin's Night Light plugin. The arc holds Settings and the three schedules, Sunset, Always On and Custom (with its hours), the one in use filled; with Custom there is also Edit Hours, which opens a sheet for the two times.
  - **Hue** (only once paired; before that the icon opens the bridge sheet to pair): the lamps on the ring, the chosen lamp's brightness in the middle and on the gauge, a slider that also turns the lamp on. The arc holds Bridge (the sheet, for the bridge address and Forget Bridge), Favourite, the lamp's On/Off switch (the starting slot) and Warm, Neutral and Cool whites for a lamp with colour temperature. Leaving the page goes back to the favourite lamp.
  - **Notifications:** All and one pill per app on the ring, with counts, filter the arc. The arc holds Clear All (Clear These under an app; confirms when there is more than one), Do Not Disturb, then the notifications, newest first, under their app's icon. The middle shows the count and the body of the notification the arc is on. Choosing a notification opens its menu: Dismiss and Dismiss All from its app.
  - **Clipboard:** All, Pinned, Text, Links, Images and Files on the ring filter the history. The arc holds History (the clipboard window), Incognito, Clear (confirms, keeps pinned), then up to 24 entries, pinned first. Choosing one copies it again and closes the dial; its menu has Copy, Pin or Unpin and Remove. Credential-like entries show as "Hidden". The middle shows the number saved and what the clipboard holds now.
  - **KDE Connect:** the paired devices on the ring, the chosen one's name in the middle and its battery on the gauge. The arc holds Settings and, while the device is reachable, what it offers: Ping, Find Phone, Send Clipboard, Send File (closes the dial for the file picker) and Send Text (a sheet with the field). The service's notice ("Ping sent") shows on the status line.
  - **Drives** (USB icon): one "Drives" pill. The arc holds Rescan, then every partition of every detachable drive, filled when mounted. Choosing a mounted one opens it and closes the dial, another mounts it; the menu has Open and Unmount or Mount, and Eject for the whole drive.
  - **GameMode:** Automatic and Forced On on the ring, Active or Idle in the middle, and the games GameMode is on for on the arc after Settings.
  - In `dialPages.js` a tile may be `lit` (drawn filled), mark where the arc starts (`start`), leave the dial open (`stay`), open a sheet (`card`, through `DialService.cardRequested`) or the shell's settings (`section`). Their actions run in `DialService.pageAction`.
  - A page's middle and gauge come from `pageView` in `DialWindow.qml` (with `slider`, the gauge takes presses: `gaugeAt` and `onGauge` in `dialLogic.js`, `DialService.setSlider`), its ring and arc from `pageView` in `DialWindow.qml`, its ring and arc from `ringLabels`, `ringIndex`, `setRing` and `items` in `DialService`, and its tiles' behaviour from `runAction` and `actionOn`.
  - **Sheets:** what a page cannot hold, a text field mostly, goes in a sheet over the disc below the pill (`statusMenu` in `DialWindow.qml`). Every sheet is a `dial/DialSheet.qml`: an icon in a disc of the sheet's colour, a title and a status line, centred as the page's readout, then its rows (`dial/DialField.qml`, a pill-shaped text field as the Wi-Fi password's) and a row of `dial/DialButton.qml` pills, the main one filled. A tile with `card: "<icon id>"` opens the sheet registered under that id in `statusMenu.components`. Esc, a press outside, Cancel or Done go back to the page the sheet was opened from; while it is open the rings do not turn. The three so far: `HueBridgeSheet` (address, Find Bridge and Pair, or Forget Bridge once paired; the Hue icon opens it directly until a bridge is paired, and pairing goes on to the Hue page), `NightHoursSheet` (From and To, saved together) and `SendTextSheet` (KDE Connect). To add one: write a `DialSheet`, register it in `qmldir` and `statusMenu.components`, and give a tile its `card`. The old quick-settings cards are no longer shown in the dial.
- **Scale:** every length goes through `Theme.px` and every font through `Theme.fontPx`, so the dial follows `uiScale` live. Checked by hand from 0.80 to 1.20.
- **Not built yet:** reduced-motion behaviour, which the shell does not have either.
